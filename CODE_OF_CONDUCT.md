# Code of Conduct

Leasekeep follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people community land trusts serve.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Leasekeep/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
